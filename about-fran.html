About Fran

Francine "Fran" Miller started Fran's Flowers in 1995 with one mission: make everyday moments feel meaningful through flowers. What began as a neighborhood flower stand in the Mission District has grown into a beloved local shop known for warm service and joyful arrangements.

Fran still designs seasonal collections each week and mentors the shop team on flower care, color pairing, and custom bouquet design. Her favorite arrangement style is bright, textural, and a little wild.

When you stop by, you are not just buying flowers. You are supporting a small business rooted in kindness, community, and craft.
